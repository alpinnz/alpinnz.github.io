About Me

I’m Alfin, a Senior Frontend Developer based in Malang with a degree in computer science from Institut Teknologi dan Bisnis ASIA. My experience spans web interfaces, mobile apps, and API integration, with a current focus on frontend product development.

As a Senior Frontend Developer at PT Ada Ide Langsung Jalan, I develop Smartlink products, including Smartlink Kasir and Smartlink Owner. I also contribute to Groapp Access and Groapp Accounting. Working with product and engineering teams, I turn requirements into responsive interfaces, reliable API integrations, and reusable components.

From January 2022 to July 2026, I led a frontend engineering team, planning work, mentoring engineers, reviewing code, and setting technical standards. Outside product work, I publish developer tools for localization, UI testing, and form validation, and conduct technical research based on challenges I encounter.
